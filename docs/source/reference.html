Reading a Plot

The big number is the dice roll that makes the Plot pay. The dots show how often that is: a 6 or an 8 pays on 5 rolls in 36, a 2 or a 12 on 1 in 36. A 7 pays nothing.

Circles marked A to D are the four starting seats. Houses are dealt to seats at random.

A Plot touches its neighbours in its row and the Plot directly above and below it.

A Season

  1. Harvest. Roll two dice. Each Plot with that number pays its resource to every House with a marker on it. A Keep (two markers) pays double. Seven pays nothing.
  2. Three Actions. Each House takes three Actions in seat order. Pick from the table below. A House with nothing useful to do passes, and a pass costs one Coin if you can pay.
  3. Event. Draw one Event card and resolve it.
  4. Flood (Seasons 3, 6, 9, 12). Score each Reach. Pay Dynasty bonuses and Accord Favor. Dynasty holders may die, and their Heir takes over.
